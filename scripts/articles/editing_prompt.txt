### Headline Doctrine: Recency Defense & Veruca Salt Stranger Gate
1. **Opening Thesis > Closing Plumbing:** Multi-turn R&D dialogues state the macro industry thesis at the **TOP** and spend the final 70-85% on patches, regexes, and dry Notary summaries. **NEVER** let downstream plumbing hijack the headline; the opening thesis is the **WHY**, and the patches are its **RECEIPT**.
2. **Stranger Stake (No Diary Titles):** Frame for a busy engineer, SEO lead, or founder scrolling at 60 mph asking *"What happens to MY stack or moat?"* Treat the author's site strictly as the wind tunnel—anchor on familiar surfaces (`Your Homepage`, `Your CDN`, `Your Server Logs`, `Google's Moat`, `OpenAI's Crawler`) and **NEVER** lead with internal repo codenames (`NPvg`, `trimnoir`, `GLOSSARY.md`, `foo_files.py`).
3. **60-Char Single Hook:** Pick ONE 80/20 hook (~42-62 chars, hard max 70, 0-1 colons, no 3-part syllabus lists; ignore retired list titles in older Spine rows).
4. **Five Psychological Lenses (`title_brainstorm`):** Generate 5 distinct candidates—one per bracketed tag below prefixing `rationale`—and **sort your #1 stranger-magnet into `title_brainstorm[0]`**:
   - `[Loss Aversion / Moat Threat]`: Incumbent moat, $10K vendor tax, or habit evaporating via a protocol shift.
   - `[Gatekeeper Myth Demolished]`: Vendor/agency excuse ("raw bot logs require origin access") proven false.
   - `[Forensic Wire Receipt]`: Counter-intuitive behavior caught live in sockets, HTTP headers, or benchmarks.
   - `[Contrast / Zero-Tax Leverage]`: Bloated industry default vs. a tiny, free, checkable Unix/Python/Nix/RFC primitive.
   - `[Information-Gap Anomaly]`: Concrete industry paradox or discovery demanding resolution.

### Field Specifications
- `headline_crucible`: 1 sentence each for `opening_macro_thesis`, `rejected_plumbing_trap` (the internal bug/chore explicitly rejected as headline), and `veruca_salt_stranger_stake`.
- `title_brainstorm`: 5 entries (one per lens above), champion sorted to `[0]`; each `filename` is a unique 4-7 word lowercase `[a-z0-9-]` slug ending in `.md`.
- `authors_imprint`: First-person summary linking the outward consulting/protocol thesis (Agent-First CDN Edge, RFC 9110/9111 Markdown, chained DNS/O2O wire logs, `sunpug.io`) to the checkable receipt while pre-empting the predictable vendor/gatekeeper dismissal.
- `promotional_tweet`: `<= 200` chars INCLUDING literal `[ARTICLE_URL]` on its own line (no other links) + 2-3 hashtags.
- `hero_image_prompt`: One vivid 16:9 illustration prompt riding `title_brainstorm[0]`'s metaphor, focal subject vertically centered for 1200x630 crop, dark obsidian-slate (`#111111`) extending to all edges, high-contrast graphic-novel ink-and-wash, no "safe zones"/"banners", ending verbatim with: `"Full-bleed edge-to-edge composition filling 100% of the canvas with no borders, no frames, no matting, no margins, and no text overlays or lettering."`
- `hero_image_alt`: Concise scene description (`< 125` chars, no `"`).
- `ai_editorial_take`: Archaeologist-from-the-Future lens (*"And then the surprising thing the author did next was..."*), grading Shannon surprisal vs. zero-bit consensus slop, anticipating a Kuhnian gatekeeper objection, and adding one fresh strategic angle.
- `next_step_prompts`: 1-2 concrete follow-up prompts honoring any explicit author notes.
- `yaml_updates`: `title` = `title_brainstorm[0].title` verbatim; `description` = 100-160 char stake + proof; `permalink` = `title_brainstorm[0].filename` (minus `.md`) inside `[INSERT PERMALINK PATTERN]`; `keywords` = comma-separated technical terms.
- `prepend_to_article_body`: 2 intro paragraphs (macro thesis -> hands-on session); no heading, rule, or code fence.
- `insert_subheadings`: 4-7 `## ` subheadings; `after_text_snippet` = 5-10 verbatim words from the **END** of the preceding paragraph before a blank line (never mid-paragraph, inside fences/stand-ins, or above an existing `#` heading).
