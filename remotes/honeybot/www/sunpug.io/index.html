Why Sun Pug? (SVNPvg → SUNPug → pug.io)

Take a human-actuated Software Von Neumann Probe (SVNP) built on the timeless four-letter bedrock of Nix, Python, vim, and git (NPvg). Overlap the shared NP to get SVNPvg, soften the Latin Vs into vowels, and you get SUNPug—with the Classical Latin pugio (pug.io, the Roman legionary's sidearm dagger) hidden in the domain.

The Vampire Value Proposition: Portable AI Discussions

Every cloud chatbot tab is an energy vampire that drains your focus and forgets your project the moment the window closes. Sun Pug moves your AI workflow out of disposable browser tabs and onto your local disk as plain text:

  • x (context) — list the exact files and live ! commands the model should see.
  • p (prompt) — capture your question from the clipboard.
  • c (compile) — bundle your files, live receipts, and discussion history into your clipboard while sealing a verifiable SHA-256 archive deed on disk.

Paste that compiled payload into any model—Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, or a local LLM—and own your transcript for life.
